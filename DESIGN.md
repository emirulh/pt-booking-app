# Emirul Hakimi PT

Direction: an approachable, athletic personal-training booking experience for Penang. ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.

- Warm white provides a quiet reading surface; charcoal carries hierarchy; deep green marks actions and selection with high contrast.
- Arial's familiar, compact forms keep a practical service readable without remote font dependencies. Large tightly set headings provide athletic character.
- The desktop split keeps the trainer, session and price visible while the client completes a focused task. Mobile removes the repeating sidebar.
- A 4/8px spacing rhythm and restrained 8–22px radii distinguish inputs, actions and grouped content.
- Cards group the offer and active booking step. Thin borders define surfaces; only the offer uses a restrained shadow.
- EH initials are an honest local placeholder, not a fabricated portrait. Replace the SVG with an owner-supplied licensed photo when available. The image component retains a real on-error initials fallback.
- Motion is a 180ms position-only entrance, never a faded blank screen. Reduced-motion preferences remove it.
- Date paging shows whole cards, with month on every card and keyboard-operable previous/next controls. This intentionally replaces drag-to-scroll so dates cannot clip mid-text.
- Portfolio mode is visible throughout. The 12-hour cancellation/rescheduling policy is explicitly a demo policy. Travel beyond 10km from George Town city center requires a quote; RM35 is never shown as the full total in that case.
